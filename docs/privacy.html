Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Vett ("we", "the app") is an independent analytics and content tool for the Hyperliquid ecosystem. This policy explains what data we handle.

What Vett does

Vett analyzes publicly available on-chain trading data from Hyperliquid's public API to estimate how a wallet's advertised performance would translate to a copier. It also generates short videos summarizing that public data and publishes them to social accounts owned by the operator of this app.

Information we access

  • Public blockchain data: wallet addresses and their public trading history from Hyperliquid. This is already public and is not personal data we collect from you.
  • Telegram: if you use our Telegram bot, we store your Telegram user ID and basic usage counts (commands used, wallets checked) to measure usage. We do not store message content or contact details.
  • Social platform authorization (TikTok, YouTube, etc.): when the operator connects their own social account, we request only the permission needed to upload videos to that same account. We use the access token solely to publish the operator's own content. We do not read, collect, or store data about other users of those platforms.

How we use TikTok data

Our use of the TikTok Content Posting API is limited to uploading videos to the authenticated account that granted access. We request the video.upload (and, where audited, video.publish) scope only. We do not access, store, or share any other TikTok user data, and we comply with TikTok's Developer Terms and Community Guidelines.

What we do NOT do

We do not sell data, we do not run ads on your data, and we do not share access tokens with third parties. Authorization tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and used only to publish the operator's own videos.

Data retention & deletion

Usage counters can be deleted on request. Social authorizations can be revoked at any time from the respective platform's settings, which immediately ends our access.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Excevity/vett.

Vett is not financial advice and is not affiliated with Hyperliquid, TikTok, or YouTube.
